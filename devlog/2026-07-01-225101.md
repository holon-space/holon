## Senior Code Review: Phased Test Harness Refactoring Plan

The plan aimed to consolidate 15 capability trait implementations across test harnesses into a composed provider, but the inventory and scope were significantly understated—and several structural risks weren't surfaced.

**What happened:**

- Validated the checkout (`ypytzkwy 7a85b119`) and found the plan targets the correct tree, though harness files live under `frontends/` not `crates/`
- Discovered the actual work is **2.5× larger**: 15 capability impls exist (6 in `sut_capabilities.rs` + 9 in `sut_handle.rs`), not 6—all already have composed providers, but Phase 3's loop was scoped to 40% of the real deletions
- Uncovered 5 factual inventory errors: "persisted seed" is actually 4 seeds requiring triage; 7 slice dirs are actually 6; Phase 1's exit gate omitted critical re-exports from `pbt/mod.rs`; Phase 0's exit gate was self-contradictory (`keyword_set_survives_sut_serialize_parse` is a lib test, making "composed lib suite green" impossible); `CapMap::insert` silently overwrites (confirming real debt)
- Identified structural risks: unbounded debugging on the critical path, Phase 1 C-3 lacking decomposition, untested performance assumptions around windowed proptest cost (headless already 46.9s/16 cases), and phase deletions that could silently break `bisection_pbt` contracts

**Decisions:**

- Added escalation valve for keystone red fixes: Phase 1 may proceed against pinned-signature reds, Phases 2–3 may not
- Made every "disclosed exclusion" in Phase 1 a tracked Phase 3 blocker rather than silent debt
- Added pre-Phase 4 smoke-benchmark to validate windowed proptest cost before committing to case budgets; explicit wall-clock budget on matrix
- Chose bless-builder-asserts as default for C-1 with concrete escalation trigger

**Status:** Plan rewritten in place with all 11 hypotheses explicitly tracked; two validation tasks (windowed cost, prod-bug classification) embedded in dependent phases.
