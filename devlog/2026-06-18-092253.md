## W1: Code-health hardening for holon-frontend — archlint violations 13→0

**Context:** W1 required driving all archlint violations in `crates/holon-frontend/` to zero while keeping changes isolated to that crate. Starting point: 13 violations across underscore-params, `.ok()` coercions, and "fallback" comment-smells.

**What happened:**
