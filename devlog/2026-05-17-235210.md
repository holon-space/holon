## Senior Review: PBT Architecture Refinement with Capability Traits

The `holon-pbt-core` refactor introduced capability traits (`Clickable`, `LiveBlockSink`, `LayoutSut`, `LayoutRef`) paired with local-newtype wrappers in `holon-layout-testing`. This pattern lets variant implementations live in shared content crates while respecting Rust's orphan rule — a meaningful upgrade from the v2 assumption of per-consumer impls scattered across frontends.

**What happened:**

- Analyzed the new capability-trait + local-newtype pattern already proven in `holon-layout-testing`
- Recognized that shared impl crates (containing variant structs + impls) are now viable when capabilities are well-named and the surface generalizes
- Relaxed the "≥2 consumers in-tree" validation gate: a single-consumer leaf crate is acceptable if its capability surface is reusable and a plausible next consumer exists (precedent: `holon-layout-testing` itself)
- Split Phase 1 refold into two sub-tasks: (a) direct orphan-rule fold via local `Sut` wrapper, (b) capability fold modeled on `LayoutSut` (testing `Loro` as non-UI adversary)
- Anchored Phase 2 extraction template to `holon-layout-testing` as the gold standard
- Updated exit criteria to explicitly gate on "does the capability surface generalize beyond this PBT's domain?"

**Decisions:**

- **Capability per concern, not per subsystem**: The `Clickable`/`LiveBlockSink` pattern shows disciplined narrowing. New risk added: "capability-trait creep" — if trait count explodes without pushback, the pattern loses its leverage.
- **Defer the generalization question**: Phase 1's fold of `loro_sync_controller_pbt` acts as the test case — Loro is non-UI, so if its SUT capabilities fit cleanly into the template, the architecture scales.

**Status:** Plan v3 finalized. Phases 4–9 (bug back-test, invariant audit, CI spike, unit-test pruning) remain unchanged; the capability pattern makes those phases cheaper, not different in shape. Ready to proceed with Phase 1.
