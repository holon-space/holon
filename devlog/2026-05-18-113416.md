## Senior Code Review: Dynamic Pudding State Machine PBT Plan

The plan to migrate the editor's state machine tests to property-based testing via a heterogeneous slice architecture was critiqued as a senior reviewer. The goal was to identify load-bearing hypotheses, structural gaps, and false-green risks before committing to a 12–16 day implementation.

### What happened

- **Identified 14 critical gaps** across assumptions, specifications, and risk management—ranked the three keystone hypotheses:
  - **H4 (struct-of-impls in `StateMachineTest`)** is day-0 proof-of-concept; if it can't deliver ≥5× speedup, the entire slice-PBT framework collapses
  - **H10's spike was artificially optimistic** (TypeChars is the easiest transition); added SplitBlock to the spike to expose tree-mutation complexity
  - **H5's crate-boundary assumption breaks in Phase 5** (macro in `holon-frontend/tests/` imports from `holon-integration-tests`); added decision gate defaulting to integration-tests crate
- **Uncovered semantic gaps**: `CachingProxy` drain-once contract was vague; `live_blocks_stale` watermark coordination unspecified; where-clause invariant filtering silently drops checks without warning
- **False-green risk flagged**: pure in-memory slice cannot reproduce SQL race bugs or CDC lag; added archlint rule forcing invariants in the pure slice to also run in wide PBT
- **Added 5 new spikes** (H6b tuple compile-time, H12 macro generic-Ref retrofit, etc.) and re-estimated effort from 12–16 days to 22–25 calendar days accounting for rebase tax on a hot main branch
- **Rewrote plan in place** with explicit hypotheses, gate conditions, and rollback strategy for Phase 7+ deferral

### Challenges

- **H7's budget estimate had zero empirical basis**: relaxed from 300–800 to ≤1500 LOC; unproven matview-in-memory cost could sink the phase
- **Macro codegen surgery was glossed over**: declaring a generic `R` parameter rewrites 60 impl blocks; Phase 1 spike required to validate risk
- **Cross-cutting concerns were under-enumerated**: only `commit_active_editor_if_changed` was named; Phase 1 must audit all SUT methods and map them to trait owners

### Decisions

- **Moved H4 to day-0 critical path** with ≥5× speedup gate—validates the entire architecture before other work proceeds
- **Default Phase 5 to `holon-integration-tests` crate** to avoid reverse-dependency antipattern; blocks on explicit re-evaluation if frontend ownership is mandatory
- **Added false-green archlint rule**: invariants marked `[pure_slice_only]` fail the build—forces all pure-slice invariants into wide PBT coverage

**Status**: Plan rewritten with 14 critiques integrated, 5 new spikes added, effort re-estimated to 22–25 days, and explicit gate conditions for H4, H5, H7, and false-green risk. Ready for implementation with day-0 proof-of-concept on struct-of-impls performance.
