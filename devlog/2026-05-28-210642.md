## Senior Review of ADRs 0004–0007: Architecture Refinement

Conducted a thorough critique of the four-tier architecture ADRs (0004–0007) plus context from 0001/0003, searching 102 files for `sort_key` blast radius and edge cases.

### What happened

- **Identified four critical gaps** papered over by confident prose: domain canonicity contradiction (Tier 1 as "source of truth" vs adapters holding physical truth), quiescence under-specification (repeated race condition source), `RequiredWiring` lacking disjunction (cannot express "Loro OR Org"), and `sort_key` removal blast radius (102 files, eight direct reads, flag-day risk)
- **Surfaced six structural problems** including `Document.filename` derivation (title collisions, FS-incompatible chars, case sensitivity), Loro→Turso "bridge" placed in no explicit tier, migration requiring serialization compat plan, and hot-path indirection cost on keystroke events
- **Reframed domain tier** as a logical projection (bytes live in adapters) not a materialized struct, with explicit operational quiescence definition
- **Enhanced 0005** with `children_of_window` for cursored reads (calendar/journal scaling), explicit precondition table (cycles, self-refs, non-sibling moves), and `#[deprecated]` deprecation window to avoid flag-day churn across PRQL/render_eval/parser
- **Strengthened 0007** by replacing flat `RequiredWiring` subset with boolean expressions (`AnyStorageOf`, `AnyOf`), introducing blessed-vs-valid manifest table, and clarifying static-vs-dynamic gating (framework gates necessary conditions; generators retain dynamic guards)
- **Added "Known weaknesses" sections** to each ADR explicitly listing open questions: serialization compat, per-adapter monotonicity invariants, phase dependency DAG, and green-line test commitment through migration phases

### Challenges

- **Concurrency determinism leak**: Loro's order under concurrent `MoveAfter` is non-deterministic; ADRs conflated this with byte-level loss. Required separating "Loro convergence" (deterministic across replicas) from "user intent under edits" (inherently lossy) from "round-trip byte fidelity" (separate concern).
- **Canonicity paradox**: "Single source of truth" contradicted "authoritative adapter under current DI graph." Resolved by explicit model: domain is a consensus state across wired adapters after quiescence, not an owned struct.
- **Filename derivation as landmine**: Deterministic derivation from title locks out external renames, creates collision traps, and is FS-incompatible. Flipped to OPEN, requiring dedicated design before implementation.

### Status

All four ADRs updated with load-bearing constraints explicit. Four critical gaps now surface as "Open questions" rather than implicit assumptions. Architecture is still ambitious (6–11 phase migration) but contracts are now falsifiable; next step is phase-DAG and serialization-compat design before Phases 2–3 implementation.
