## Query Origin Instrumentation & Follow-up 6 Falsification

Investigated the two remaining LiveData migration follow-ups from the handoff: the mystery 1600 queries per test case (Follow-up 5) and the matview-skip optimization premise (Follow-up 6).

**What happened:**

- Built `SpanCollector::queries_by_origin()` to walk OTel span ancestor chains and tally queries by their calling context, reusing existing tracing infra instead of adding parallel instrumentation to `DbHandle`
- Ran instrumentation on both `sql_only` and `Full` PBT variants — found per-case totals of 125–180 reads, not the baseline 1739
- Identified that prior work (matview cache + invariant-side LiveData) already resolved the mystery; remaining hot spots are OrgSync echo-loop (~33% of Full case time) and orphaned spawned-task queries (~19%)
- Created `cdc_base_vs_matview_repro.rs` to test the Follow-up 6 premise: whether base-table writes bypass matviews and emit CDC events directly
- Proved base-table `INSERT`/`UPDATE`/`DELETE` emit zero CDC batches — matviews are the only CDC source, falsifying the optimization premise

**Challenges:**

- Initial approach added counters to `DbHandle` and `BackendEngine`; pivoted to OTel span analysis after recognizing `tracing::instrument` and `SpanCollector` already provided the infrastructure
- Per-transition span reset meant `Drop` only saw the last transition's metrics; solved by accumulating snapshots before each reset

**Decisions:**

- Kept the instrumentation as durable test infra despite resolving the mystery — `queries_by_origin()` catches regressions
- Preserved the CDC reproducer as characterization documentation that matviews are the required mechanism

**Status:** Both follow-ups closed (F5 resolved by prior work, F6 falsified). Instrumentation and reproducer committed. Ready to move to next handoff items.
