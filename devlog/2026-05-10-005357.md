## Fixed E2E PBT bug in send_leader_chord

The Phase 2 handoff flagged a divergence in property-based testing: `NavigateBack` keystrokes were typing `" b"` into the journals page content, corrupting the block state. The bug was traced to a conflict between the PBT helper and Phase 2's new reactive engine.

- **Root cause identified**: `send_leader_chord` sends raw keystrokes `" "` (leader) and `"b"` (chord key) to the driver. After Phase 2 added `ReactiveEngineDriver::send_raw_keystroke` for atomic editor primitives, these keystrokes route into the focused editor's `MutableText` mirror. When an earlier `ClickBlock(block:journals)` focused the journals editor, the chord typed `" b"` directly into `block:journals.content`, diverging from the expected navigation operation.
- **Fix implemented** across three files:
  - `crates/holon-frontend/src/user_driver.rs:299` — added trait method `dispatches_chords_via_raw_keystroke()` (default `false`)
  - `frontends/tui/src/user_driver.rs:663` — TUI driver overrides to `true` (its `send_raw_keystroke` routes through chord resolution)
  - `crates/holon-integration-tests/src/pbt/sut.rs:3091` — `send_leader_chord` consults the new method; headless drivers dispatch navigation intent directly via `synthetic_dispatch("navigation", nav_op, …)` instead of raw keystrokes
- **Verified**: Both PBT variants pass — `general_e2e_pbt_sql_only` in 509s, `general_e2e_pbt` (Full, the previously-failing variant) in 559s with `PROPTEST_CASES=1 PROPTEST_MAX_SHRINK_ITERS=0`
- **Devlog written**: `devlog/2026-05-10-leader-chord-headless-typing-fix.md`

**Status**: Bug fixed and verified. Ready to proceed with Phase 2 final cleanup or commit the ~50 uncommitted files (Phase 1 cells + Phase 2 authority flip + today's fix).
