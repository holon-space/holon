## Resolved Turso dependency conflicts across ICU and IDNA crates

The workspace had cascading version conflicts between the turso fork and existing dependencies, stemming from incompatible ICU 2.x sub-crate versions and IDNA adapter pins.

**What happened:**

- Attempted `cargo update turso` but hit `icu_normalizer` version conflict: turso_core required `~2.2.0` while workspace-hack was locked to `2.1.1`
- Regenerated workspace-hack with `cargo hakari generate` to resync dependency pins
- Hit a second conflict with `zerovec`: `icu_collator v2.2.0` required `^0.11.6` but `icu_locale`'s transitive dep was locked to `0.11.5`
- Root cause emerged: `idna_adapter v1.2.1` pinned `icu_normalizer ^2` (locked at 2.1.1) while turso_core's `icu_collator` needed `~2.2.0`
- Resolved by updating `zerovec`, `turso`, and `idna_adapter` together: `cargo update zerovec turso idna_adapter`
- This bumped idna_adapter to v1.2.2 and elevated the entire ICU suite from 2.1.x → 2.2.x consistently across all transitive deps

**Decisions:**

- Updated `idna_adapter` explicitly rather than waiting for hakari to pick it up, since the core conflict was in its version constraint
- Let Cargo resolve all 31 downstream packages at once rather than piecemeal updates

**Status:** Turso updated successfully to the holon branch (commit 73d59b02); Cargo.lock now has consistent ICU 2.2.0 and zerovec 0.11.6 across all dependency trees.
