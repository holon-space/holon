## Goal
Unify PBT transitions onto the generic `holon_pbt_core` trait vocabulary so a single transition struct is reusable across PBTs/mediums (E2E, layout, editor-pure, future Flutter), reducing the cost of adding/testing new functionality.

## Architecture (current end-state)
Three generic traits in `crates/holon-pbt-core/src/lib.rs`:
- `TransitionFactory<Ref>` — `weighted_generator` (+ assoc `Reason`).
- `TransitionRef<Ref>` — `preconditions` + `apply_to_ref` (S-independent; + assoc `Reason`).
- `TransitionImpl<Ref, Sut: ?Sized>` — `apply_to_sut` only.

The E2E enum `E2ETransition` (generated by `declare_e2e_transitions!` in `crates/holon-integration-tests/src/pbt/transition_dispatch.rs`) hand-rolls a `match` dispatch (NOT `declarative_enum_dispatch` — it can't dispatch a foreign generic trait):
- `impl TransitionRef<ReferenceState> for E2ETransition` (no S).
- `impl<S: SutHandle> TransitionImpl<ReferenceState, S> for E2ETransition`.
- `impl SqlBudget for E2ETransition` (otel-gated; `SqlBudget` trait lives in `transition_budgets.rs`).

`SutHandle` (transition_dispatch.rs) is now **native-async** (no `#[async_trait]`), dispatched as **concrete `S: SutHandle`** (runner passes `&mut E2ESut<V>`, no `dyn`). It is a **supertrait bundle**: `pub trait SutHandle: SutEditorMirrorWrite + SutBlockTreeWrite`. This lets a transition narrow its `apply_to_sut` bound to a fine cap while the enum's `S: SutHandle` still satisfies it.

`CapBlockId` (capabilities.rs) is now `pub use holon_api::EntityUri;` (alias retired to the real type); `holon-pbt-core` gained a `holon-api` dep.
