### Item 1: Deterministic Caret Bug (FIXED & VERIFIED)
**Root cause:** On chord-dispatched structural ops (Indent/Outdent/MoveUp/MoveDown), the reference model unconditionally re-opened the editor with caret at end-of-text (modeling a pre-chord click). Headless drivers never click, creating divergence after `SplitBlock` (caret at 0) + `Outdent`: ref said caret=5, SUT correctly said 0. GPUI driver also clicked even when already focused, re-seating the caret at element center.

**Fix:**
- `crates/holon-integration-tests/src/pbt/transitions/mod.rs`: Added `RefEditorMirror` bound to helper, skip editor re-open when block's editor already active.
- `frontends/gpui/src/user_driver.rs`: Skip click-to-focus when target already has focus.

**Verification:** `general_e2e_pbt_sql_only` went from red to green (57s). Full/Loro gate stays green (61s). Editor pure slice 3/3. GPUI capture replay completed all steps with zero panics.

**Status in worktree:** Superseded by main's concurrent chord-caret fix during rebase. Both versions are complementary; reconciliation already happened.
