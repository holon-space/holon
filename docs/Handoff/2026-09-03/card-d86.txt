# D86 Production iroh pairing has NO read/write capability dimension — route its imports through `admit`, or give enrollment a capability?
> Lane pair-prod (report `.claude/worktrees/pair-prod/lane-report-pair-prod.md` §2a, §6) measured that D72.a's "ONE acceptor boundary" is two today: `holon_sharing::acceptor::admit` (the function with the Read/Write rule and the full refusal taxonomy) has NO production caller — its only call site is the relay/envelope leg (`holon-sharing/src/sync.rs:232`). The production iroh leg authorises in `share_enrollment::acceptor_enroll` (`iroh_advertiser.rs:171-177`), which proves possession of a `CapabilitySecret` and is all-or-nothing. Consequence: a device paired over production iroh under a READ-ONLY cert can write, because nothing on that path ever reads the cert. The red test `a_read_only_pairing_cannot_write_back_to_the_owner` pins this and stays `#[ignore]`d until ruled. Related fact (§2b): whole-store replication over iroh is correct only by vacuity — `replicate_all` takes ONE roster bound to ONE tree id, so a second registered container could not authenticate; per-container rosters are the prerequisite for the first extra container.
? One boundary means one function decides; two gates that disagree are a bypass by construction. The fix must make the iroh leg's decision the same code that the relay leg runs.
* a | Route the iroh leg's imports through `admit`: after `acceptor_enroll` proves possession, every imported batch on the iroh connection passes `admit` with the peer's cert chain (capability from the chain, container binding, audience) before it reaches the doc; `acceptor_enroll` keeps transport-level proof only | +ONE decision function for both wires (D72.a as ruled); the existing refusal taxonomy and the admit-fuzz PBT cover the iroh leg for free | -per-batch cost on the hot path (cert chain parse once per connection, then a cheap check); the enrollment proof and the cert chain must be bound to the same identity (a new invariant to pin)
- b | Give enrollment a capability dimension: two secrets per share (read secret / write secret), `acceptor_enroll` grants the capability the presented secret proves; imports on a read-only connection are refused at the transport | +cheapest per-batch path; no per-batch admit | -a SECOND authorisation vocabulary beside the cert chain (the relay leg keeps `admit`); two secrets to distribute and rotate; D72.a's "one boundary" stays unmet
- c | Defer: ship pairing read/write-only (every paired device is a full writer), keep the test ignored, revisit before the first read-only share ships | +unblocks pair-prod now | -a known authz bypass lives in main with a name; contradicts D72.a
